<p>A suppression list is the set of email addresses and domains you do not want hubsell to contact: competitors, or anyone else who should stay out of your outreach. Anything on the list is skipped when a flow sends.</p>

<div class="kb-callout kb-callout--tip">
  <p>Add an <b>email address</b> to block one person. Add a <b>domain</b> to block everyone at that company.</p>
</div>

<h2 class="kb-sub">Add an email or domain to the suppression list</h2>
<ol class="kb-steps">
  <li>

      <figcaption>Opening the profile menu</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>Click <span class="kb-kbd">Suppression</span>.</p>

      <img src="https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-08-25/ab700b82-07e6-4fd3-8a1f-fa7947edbfc3/user_cropped_screenshot_8b09cbcf24634e82a0cc089e3039355e_text_export.jpeg" alt="Suppression in the profile menu" loading="lazy" onerror="this.closest('.kb-figure').classList.add('is-missing')" />

<p>Team members get their own login, and can be picked as the sending user when contacts are added to a flow.</p>

<h2 class="kb-sub">Send an invitation</h2>
<ol class="kb-steps">
  <li>
    <p>Click your <b>profile icon</b>.</p>
    <figure class="kb-figure" data-ph="Screenshot: the profile icon">

      <figcaption>Opening the profile menu</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>Click <span class="kb-kbd">Company Settings</span>.</p>
    <figure class="kb-figure" data-ph="Screenshot: Company Settings">
      <img src="https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-08-25/e6dc515a-6bd2-4f57-bd1e-097f4b352ae8/user_cropped_screenshot_48e9fc205ef64aab8118225eb56db805_text_export.jpeg" alt="Company Settings in the profile menu" loading="lazy" onerror="this.closest('.kb-figure').classList.add('is-missing')" />
      <figcaption>Opening Company Settings</figcaption>
    </figure>

  <li>
    <p>Click <span class="kb-kbd">Team Management</span>.</p>
    <figure class="kb-figure" data-ph="Screenshot: the Team Management tab">
      <img src="https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-08-25/40f81ee6-752c-4c34-9097-cea0227d556f/user_cropped_screenshot_e3152866524b4f76983129c0dd09f86d_text_export.jpeg" alt="The Team Management tab" loading="lazy" onerror="this.closest('.kb-figure').classList.add('is-missing')" />
      <figcaption>Opening Team Management</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>Click <span class="kb-kbd">Invite your team</span>.</p>
    <figure class="kb-figure" data-ph="Screenshot: Invite your team">
      <img src="https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-08-25/3646b385-5873-4495-97c7-92e97edd26ff/user_cropped_screenshot_c6a2d9f07f5a4fa78110f3818dd05daa_text_export.jpeg" alt="The Invite your team button" loading="lazy" onerror="this.closest('.kb-figure').classList.add('is-missing')" />

</ol>

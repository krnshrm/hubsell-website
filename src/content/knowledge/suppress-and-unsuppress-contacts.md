<p>Suppression is how you take individual contacts out of your outreach without deleting them. A suppressed contact stays in your account and keeps its data, but flows skip it when they send.</p>

<div class="kb-callout kb-callout--tip">
  <p>This is different from the <a href="/knowledge/suppression-list">suppression list</a>, which blocks email addresses and whole domains before they ever reach your contacts. Use that for competitors, and this for individual people already in your lists.</p>
</div>

<div class="kb-callout kb-callout--warn">
  <p class="kb-callout__h">Common mistake</p>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Suppressing just before outreach.</b> Contacts have already been enriched by that point, so credits are spent on people who will never be contacted. Suppressing at or before enrichment is recommended, though not required.</li>
  </ul>

<p>Lists are how you organise contacts for finding and filtering. Moving contacts between them is a tidying action: it changes where a contact lives, not what happens to it.</p>

<div class="kb-callout kb-callout--tip">
  <p>A contact sits in <b>one list at a time</b>. Moving it to another list removes it from the one it was in.</p>
</div>
